# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-14)

First public release, accompanying the paper *Serviceability-oriented
multimodal data integration for prescriptive road-tunnel maintenance digital
twins* (Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology).

- Maintenance ontology (TBox), RADS and COBie alignment modules and the
  standards-governance module.
- FMEA ABox with the six reviewed AASHTO DCRT Chapter 16 chains and the
  demonstration defect records.
- SPARQL realisations of all 31 competency questions (Appendix E), with a
  runner script and tests.
- Streamlit reference build (Section 5.2) with Docker packaging.
- Synthetic demonstration data for two fictitious tunnels.
